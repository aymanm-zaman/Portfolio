Overview

UT Baja Racing is building the University of Toronto's first-ever Baja SAE competition vehicle. As Co-Lead of the Brake Team, I'm responsible for the complete braking system subassembly — from initial requirements derivation through detailed design and system integration.

The system must satisfy Baja SAE competition regulations while performing reliably under the dynamic loads and thermal conditions of off-road racing.

Analysis & Design

Performance requirements were derived through analytical calculations covering:

  • Static structural loads on brake pedal, caliper mounts, and rotor
  • Dynamic braking force distribution front-to-rear under deceleration
  • Torsional stiffness requirements for the pedal assembly
  • Thermal performance of rotor and caliper under sustained braking

All calculations were performed in Microsoft Excel, benchmarked against prior SAE Baja competition data and published research literature to validate assumptions and system specifications.

CAD & Integration

The brake pedal assembly is being designed in SolidWorks based on the geometric and load constraints derived analytically. Component selection (calipers, rotors, master cylinder, hardware) is driven by the calculated specifications and regulatory requirements.

I coordinate internal team meetings and task assignments, and collaborate externally with other subsystem leads — chassis, suspension, drivetrain — to ensure smooth system integration across the full vehicle.
