LM324N Signal Conditioning PCB — MIE346

Designed and laid out a two-layer PCB for a multi-stage op-amp signal conditioning circuit, taking the design from schematic capture through KiCad layout, DRC, and Gerber generation. Built in collaboration with Sayali (class of 2T6).

4 Op-amp stages (LM324N)
2 Copper layers
15+ Passive components

PCB Layout

Schematic

Course MIE346 — Electronics for Mechanical Engineers
Collaborator Sayali (Class of 2T6)
Tools KiCad, LTspice
IC LM324N Quad Op-Amp

Circuit Overview

The circuit conditions two signals — a current sense input (ISENSE) and a reference voltage — into two calibrated output channels (VOUT1 and VOUT2). Current is measured via R15, a 0.1 Ω shunt resistor, giving a voltage proportional to current. All four op-amp sections of the LM324N are used across two parallel processing chains.

Signal Chain

The VOUT1 chain (IC1A → IC1B) handles current sense amplification. IC1A acts as a differential amplifier summing the shunt signal with a voltage reference set by the R2/R3/R4/R5 divider. IC1B applies a second gain stage with frequency roll-off via the C4 feedback capacitor, producing a filtered output on VOUT1.

The VOUT2 chain (IC1C → IC1D) processes the VOUT2 reference signal. IC1C applies inverting gain with R9 (100k) feedback; IC1D provides additional scaling through R11 (13k) and R12 (6.8k). Both chains use small capacitors (C1, C2, C5, C6) for high-frequency noise rejection.

PCB Design

The two-layer board was laid out in KiCad. The top copper layer (red) carries signal traces; the bottom layer (blue) handles return paths and cross-unders. Key layout decisions included:

  • Keeping signal traces short between the connector (JP1) and the first op-amp stage to minimise parasitic capacitance
  • Placing decoupling capacitors (C3–C6) close to IC power pins
  • Routing the GND plane on the bottom layer for a clean return path
  • Ensuring adequate copper clearance for the +19V supply traces

The design passed KiCad's DRC checks and Gerber files were generated for fabrication.

Component Selection

  • LM324N — chosen for its single-supply operation (works from +3V to +32V), four op-amps in one package, and availability
  • R15 = 0.1 Ω — low-value shunt minimises power loss while producing a measurable voltage drop at the expected current range
  • Resistor values in the gain networks were calculated from the required transfer function, then selected to the nearest E96 standard values
